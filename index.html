PROJECT 01 · THERMAL ENGINEERING · CFD · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Forced-Convection Thermal Management of an Embedded System

Numerical investigation of the thermal behaviour and cooling strategies of a Raspberry Pi 4 embedded computing system under forced convection. The study combines computational fluid dynamics, heat-transfer analysis and airflow investigation to evaluate thermal-management performance.

System Raspberry Pi 4
Domain Thermal Engineering
Method Computational Fluid Dynamics
Software SimScale
Turbulence Model k-ω SST
Air Velocity 1–5 m/s

Research Question

How does forced convection influence the thermal behaviour of an embedded computing system? The study investigates the relationship between airflow conditions, temperature distribution and cooling performance.

Objectives

01 Thermal Analysis
02 Airflow Analysis
03 CFD Investigation
04 Cooling Performance

CFD Methodology

Geometry definition → material and thermal properties → heat sources → boundary conditions → computational mesh → CFD solution → post-processing and scientific interpretation.

Simulation Results

Key Result

Initial CPU Temperature 69.8 °C Reference thermal condition
Cooled CPU Temperature 53.9 °C Simulated enhanced forced-convection condition

The simulated CPU temperature decreases from 69.8 °C to 53.9 °C under the enhanced forced-convection condition, illustrating the thermal-management effect investigated in the numerical study.
